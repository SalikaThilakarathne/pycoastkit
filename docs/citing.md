# Citing and data sources

## Citing pycoastkit

If `pycoastkit` contributes to published work, please cite the package:

> Thilakarathne, S. (2026). *pycoastkit: a toolkit for sandy coasts* (version 0.1.0).
> Helmholtz-Zentrum Hereon. https://github.com/SalikaThilakarathne/pycoastkit

## Data sources

**Waves (NOWPHAS).** Nationwide Ocean Wave information network for Ports and
HArbourS, Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT),
Japan; data observed by the MLIT Ports and Harbours Bureau and processed by
the Port and Airport Research Institute (PARI).
<https://nowphas.mlit.go.jp/>. Reuse is free with attribution; state any
processing applied.

**Beach profiles (HORS).** Hasaki Oceanographical Research Station, Port and
Airport Research Institute. Please cite:

- Banno, M., Nakamura, S., Kosako, T., Nakagawa, Y., Yanagishima, S. and
  Kuriyama, Y. (2020). Long-term observations of beach variability at
  Hasaki, Japan. *Journal of Marine Science and Engineering* 8, 871.
  <https://doi.org/10.3390/jmse8110871>

## Methods

- Dolan, R. and Davis, R. E. (1992). An intensity scale for Atlantic coast
  northeast storms. *Journal of Coastal Research* 8(4), 840–853.
  (Storm power.)
- Häfner, D., Gemmrich, J. and Jochum, M. (2021). Real-world rogue wave
  probabilities. *Scientific Reports* 11, 10084. (Spectral sea-state
  parameters.)

## Coastal science

- Roelvink, D. and Reniers, A. (2012). *A Guide to Modeling Coastal
  Morphology*. Advances in Coastal and Ocean Engineering, Vol. 12. World
  Scientific, Singapore. ISBN 978-981-4304-25-2. (Background for the
  [glossary](glossary.md): waves, nearshore currents, sediment transport,
  bar dynamics, dune erosion and coastal profile models.)
- Dean, R. G. (1973). Heuristic models of sand transport in the surf zone.
  *Proceedings of the Conference on Engineering Dynamics in the Surf Zone*,
  Sydney, 208–214. (Equilibrium beach profile.)
- Sallenger, A. H. (2000). Storm impact scale for barrier islands. *Journal
  of Coastal Research* 16(3), 890–895. (Storm impact regimes.)
